# ABOUT THE INTERIA COLLECTIVE · À PROPOS DU COLLECTIF INTERIA

**InterIA Mathematical Collective** is a small, open group dedicated to
clear, reproducible mathematical writing at the interface of analysis,
number theory, and signal methods. We favor (i) modular LaTeX sources,
(ii) honest scope statements, (iii) CI-backed builds.

> “From analysis to clarity: a proof of the framework — not of Riemann.”

## Origins · Genèse

🇬🇧 This project was initiated to honor **Bernard Couret** and to help preserve,
organize, and share rigorous material curated by the Couret family.
We see mathematics as a shared, human endeavor: careful structure,
transparent bounds, and calm language.

🇫🇷 Ce projet rend hommage à **Bernard Couret** et s’inscrit dans la préservation
et la transmission de matériaux rigoureux organisés par la famille Couret.
Notre fil conducteur : structure soignée, bornes transparentes, langue apaisée.

## Mission

- Reproducible analytic frameworks (LaTeX + CI).
- Proof-only deliverables with explicit scope.
- Bilingual summaries for wider access.
