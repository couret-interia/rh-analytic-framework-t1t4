\section*{About the Author}

\textbf{InterIA Mathematical Collective — Couret – Unification Series, Vol.~1.}
This repository inaugurates a series of open, reproducible mathematical
artefacts led by Alexandre Couret and collaborators, formalizing
analytic proof frameworks around classical number–theoretic problems.

\medskip

\noindent The present volume packages the T1$′$–T4 analytic chain
(explicit formula, weighted frame bound, wave–packet localization,
cross–positivity) as a journal–ready LaTeX project with complete proofs
and build recipes. It is a \emph{framework proof}, not a resolution of RH.
